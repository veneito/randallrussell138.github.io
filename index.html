Family Dashboard: Privacy Policy

Family Dashboard is a private app used only by members of our family. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

When a family member connects their Google account, the app can read and update their Google Calendar events, and access files in the family's shared Google Drive folder. It uses this only to show and manage calendars and family files inside the dashboard.

Storage

Data stays on our family's own computer. Google sign-in tokens are stored there and never shared.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or send anyone's data to third parties, and we don't use it for advertising.

Removing access

Anyone can disconnect Google in the dashboard's Settings, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
